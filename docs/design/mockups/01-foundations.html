khronoz foundations

Flat, light, product first

White is the material. Hierarchy comes from type and rules, not from boxes. Every grey is a pure grey; color lives in the data and in one accent. Everything on this sheet is live in the current accent and mode; switch them bottom right.

Type

One family. Plus Jakarta Sans carries the wordmark, the titles and the interface, at 400 500 600 700 and 800. Titles are 700; interface text is 400, 500 and 600. Negative tracking starts at 20px and grows with the size, because the face opens up as it gets larger. Sentence case throughout; no all-caps, no eyebrows, no monospace, no italic.

Fallback stack "Plus Jakarta Sans", "Segoe UI", system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif. One Bunny request: fonts.bunny.net/css?family=plus-jakarta-sans:400,500,600,700,800. Measured, and worth knowing: this face ships no tabular-figure feature, so font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums is declared on the body but is a no-op here. Numeric alignment is structural instead: every column of figures is right-aligned or centred in its own cell, so nothing goes ragged.

The four token sets

Two accents times two modes. Every neutral is a pure achromatic grey — R, G and B are equal on all of them, in both modes and under both accents. The only hues on a screen are the accent, the eight-slot chip ramp and the fault, attention and positive tints. The sidebar carries no accent tint; the active nav item still does. Ratios are measured, light then dark. Control borders clear 3:1 on the canvas and on the sidebar; every text pair clears 4.5:1 on both.

The chip ramp

Eight flat oklch hues. Every hue keeps 35° clear of the accent and 20° clear of fault red, so a chip never reads as an error and never competes with a button. Slot 8 is the only slot that moves when the accent changes: under the blue accent it rotates from 252° to 320°. Text on fill and text on the ground are measured.

Validation

Already taken

Both columns are 60px tall. The label row is display:flex; justify-content:space-between, so the error takes space that already exists and nothing below it moves. Copy stays to one line: Required. Enter a valid email. Already taken. At least 8 characters. Passwords don’t match. Choose at least one.

Errors with no field of their own render as a flat fault-soft banner above the fields: one sentence, plus the fix. Reset your password.

Agency users see no switcher above the agency: they have one agency. Platform users get the switcher there, and the same footer as everyone.

Today, drawn on the one scale the whole product uses: a day that opens at 06:00 and closes at 30:00. Ticks every hour, taller at 06, 12, 18, 24 and 30; the accent marks now and says the time; the headcount on duty at that moment sits on the right — 147 here, which is the afternoon, standard and flexi lanes of the on-duty chart added up.
