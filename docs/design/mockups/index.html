Flat, light, product first. One family, pure neutrals, one accent. Scheduling and daily time records for Philippine government agencies, under Civil Service Commission rules.

White is the material. Hierarchy comes from type and rules, never from boxes. A bordered panel appears only where grouping needs a container.

Every grey is a pure grey; color lives in the data and in one accent. Shift chips, status pills, lane bars and avatars carry the hue; the accent carries the one action. Every color is joined by a word or a letter.

The timetable is the hero. A workday here can run to 30:00, so the roster draws night runs past midnight and the 24-hour rule appears in the sidebar, the chart axis and the day header.
