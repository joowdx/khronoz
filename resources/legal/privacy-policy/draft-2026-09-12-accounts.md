This notice explains personal information handled by khronoz, a scheduling and timekeeping platform. It applies to account users and people whose employment records an employer places in the platform, including employees who do not have an account. Your employer must also provide its own notice explaining its employment purposes, legal grounds, recipients and retention arrangements.

## Who is responsible

Your employer or agency generally determines why and how employment information is used and acts as its personal information controller. Khronoz processes those records under the employer's instructions and remains responsible for applicable processor duties. For purposes the operator independently determines, such as administering platform accounts and protecting the service, the operator acts as controller. The agency processing agreement must describe these roles and instructions; this notice does not replace it.

## Information and purposes

- **Accounts and access:** name, login email, password hash, invitation and verification state, permissions, agency membership and an optional link to an employee record. These enable invitations, authentication, recovery and authorized access. We also record which versions of this notice and the User Agreement you acknowledged and when.
- **Employment and scheduling:** employee number and name, position, agency labels, placement history, assigned schedules, roster, exemptions and authorized overtime. These establish expected work and support attendance administration. Employee profiles do not request sex, birthdate, email or mobile number. A login account separately requires email.
- **Attendance:** terminal and enrollment identifiers, recorded punch times and device codes, import details, corrections or voiding reasons, calculated attendance, daily time records and approval history. These support calculations, review and record integrity.
- **Documents and verification:** frozen daily time records, attestation names, roles and times, verification tokens and optional archived PDFs with storage metadata. Platform attestations are application records, not certificate-backed digital signatures.
- **Operation and support:** session identifiers, cookies, connection information such as IP address and browser information where recorded by the configured session or server systems, and necessary diagnostic or support records. These help operate and secure the service. Legal acknowledgments do not add a separate IP-address or browser-history log.

Information comes from you, your employer's authorized staff, attendance terminals and imported records. This version imports punch records and terminal identifiers; it does not store fingerprint images or biometric templates on the server. Ask your employer about any biometric processing performed by its attendance devices.

Do not put diagnoses, government identification numbers, passwords or unrelated personal details in tags, remarks or attendance notes. Necessary leave or other sensitive information requires the employer to establish an applicable legal ground and safeguards.

## Optional sign-in methods and account changes

You may connect Google or Apple from your activated account settings when the provider is enabled. The provider tells us an account identifier and may supply an identifying email, including an Apple relay address. We store the provider, identifier, optional email, connection date and last-use date to recognize that connection. We do not create an account or connect one merely because email addresses match. Provider tokens are used temporarily to verify the response and are not saved as account credentials. We do not retain provider avatars or raw profile responses.

Choosing a provider sends you to that provider and tells it that you are accessing Khronoz. Its own privacy notice and account controls apply to its processing. Disconnecting removes the association in Khronoz; it does not revoke permissions inside the provider. Password sign-in remains available, and employer records are not sent to a provider as part of these sign-in flows.

If you add a passkey, we store its name, credential identifier, public key, verification metadata, creation date and last-use date. Your device or passkey manager controls the private key and verifies your screen lock. Khronoz does not receive your device PIN, fingerprint or face data. Your chosen device or passkey manager may synchronize passkeys under its own arrangements. You can rename and remove registered passkeys in Security settings.

Optional authenticator-app two-factor authentication stores an encrypted setup secret, encrypted recovery codes and confirmation state. Codes verify sign-in and sensitive changes; a recovery code is consumed after use. You can regenerate recovery codes or disable this method after confirming your identity. Password and social sign-ins require this second factor when enabled; a device-verified passkey satisfies sign-in directly.

Settings let you change your account name and request a login-email replacement. We retain the pending address, a verification-token hash and expiry while the request remains pending; an expired request cannot change your email. Your verified address stays active until the replacement is confirmed, after which we notify the old address. You can cancel or resend a pending request. Changing a password cancels a pending email change and invalidates other browser sessions and remembered logins; it does not disable two-factor authentication.

Short-lived server-side challenges support these operations: identity confirmation and pending second-factor sign-in last five minutes; passkey ceremonies expire after one minute; provider state expires after five minutes; Apple's encrypted callback relay expires after two minutes. Email verification links expire after one hour. Expiry prevents use; storage cleanup and backups follow the operational retention arrangements below. Credential management concerns your own login account and does not change employer records or permissions.

## Legal grounds and your choices

The responsible controller must identify an applicable ground for each purpose. Account administration may be necessary to provide the service requested; security processing requires an appropriate, assessed ground. Employment processing may be necessary under applicable obligations, contracts or public functions, depending on the employer and purpose. Sensitive information requires a ground permitted specifically for that information.

Acknowledging this notice confirms that it was presented to you. It is not permission for every use of your information. Where optional processing depends on consent, it needs a separate, specific choice and a way to withdraw it. Declining optional consent does not remove your statutory rights or independently justified employment-retention obligations. No optional marketing consent is included in the acknowledgment screen.

## Access and sharing

Access within the platform depends on agency membership and assigned permissions. Authorized employer personnel and platform operators may access information as needed for their responsibilities. Hosting, storage, delivery and support providers may process information under appropriate arrangements. Disclosures required by law or necessary for properly assessed legal claims are handled under applicable requirements.

**Anyone who has a daily time record's public verification link can view the frozen record without signing in.** It contains the employee's identity and employment details, attendance and related exemption information, totals and attestations. Search-engine exclusion does not make that link private. Employers must assess this disclosure, inform employees and share links only with appropriate recipients. A recipient may retain a copy. PDF downloading is separately permission-controlled. Public verification exists whether or not the employer enables private PDF archiving.

## Account deletion and employment records

A request to delete your khronoz account concerns access to the platform and account information no longer required. It does not automatically delete employee profiles, attendance, daily time records or employment documents maintained by your employer. Information needed to preserve lawful historical attestations may require separate assessment.

You retain applicable privacy rights over employment records. Your employer assesses requests against the purpose, lawful grounds, retention requirements and legal holds. Requests received by khronoz about employer-controlled records will be referred to the employer for assessment and appropriate action. They are not rejected simply because they concern employment records.

There is currently no self-service account-deletion or employment-record-erasure feature. Employee removal from active lists is not secure erasure. Contact your employer's privacy representative for employment requests and the operator's privacy contact for account requests. Before publication, these contacts and the assisted request procedure must be completed: [[WORKING CONTACTS AND REQUEST PROCEDURE]].

## Your rights and requests

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request information about processing, access, correction, objection, blocking or erasure, and portability where applicable. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent. You may also seek remedies and complain to the [National Privacy Commission](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-subject-rights/).

Identify the records and the outcome you seek. The responsible party may verify identity and authority using only information reasonably needed, explain any lawful refusal or limitation, and describe available next steps. Khronoz assists the employer with requests concerning its records under the agreed processing arrangements. Acknowledgment of this notice does not waive these rights.
