## Using khronoz

Khronoz provides scheduling, attendance administration and daily time records for authorized agencies and employers. Accounts are issued by invitation. Use the service only within the authority and permissions your employer or the operator grants you. Accepting this agreement concerns your platform use; it does not replace your employment terms or an agency's service and processing agreements.

## Accounts and security

Provide accurate account information, protect credentials and use only your own account. Do not share access, impersonate another person, bypass agency boundaries or permissions, or attempt to alter protected history. Report suspected unauthorized access through [[SECURITY CONTACT]]. An administrator's invitation does not accept this agreement for you.

Access may be restricted or ended when authorization ends, an account is compromised, or service misuse requires action. The responsible party should explain the reason and available remedy where appropriate and legally permitted. Ending access does not automatically erase records that have a continuing lawful purpose.

## Records and responsibilities

Enter accurate information and correct mistakes through the supported correction and review procedures. Do not fabricate punches, approvals or employment records. Submit only information necessary for the authorized task; avoid unrelated personal details and sensitive information in free-text fields.

Your employer determines authorized users, employment rules, employee notices, lawful processing grounds, retention and approval responsibilities. It must review calculated results before using them for pay, employment decisions or official submissions. Calculations depend on the records and rules supplied and may require correction. The platform does not itself establish an employee's legal entitlement or certify compliance with every employment rule.

An attestation records the acting user, role and time. It must be made only by an authorized person who has reviewed the record. Current platform attestations are not certificate-backed digital signatures.

## Documents and verification links

An attested daily time record may have a public verification link. Anyone holding that link can see its frozen employee, attendance and attestation details without an account. Share it only for an appropriate, authorized purpose. Private PDF archiving is optional and does not control whether the HTML verification link exists. Recipients may retain copies outside the platform.

## Privacy and requests

The [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy) explains data handling. Acknowledging it is separate from accepting this agreement and does not provide blanket consent for optional processing. Neither document waives statutory privacy rights.

An account-deletion request concerns platform access and account information no longer needed. It does not automatically erase employee profiles, attendance or other employer-controlled records. Employees retain applicable rights over those records. The employer assesses access, correction, objection, blocking and erasure requests against lawful purposes and retention duties; khronoz refers employment-record requests to the employer and assists appropriately.

Self-service account deletion and employment-record erasure are not available in this version. Use the assisted request process described in the Privacy Policy. Do not assume that removing an employee from active lists deletes historical records.

## Availability and appropriate use

Do not disrupt the service, access another agency's information without authority, introduce malicious content or use records for unrelated purposes. The operator may maintain or update the service and address misuse. Availability and support arrangements for an agency belong in its service agreement: [[SERVICE CONTACT AND ARRANGEMENTS]]. Keep required organizational backups and records under your employer's approved procedures.

Each party remains responsible for its own duties under applicable law. Nothing here excludes rights, liabilities or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded. Any additional service commitments must be stated in the applicable agency agreement; this draft does not invent them.

Philippine law applies, subject to mandatory protections and the jurisdiction of competent authorities. Raise service concerns through [[SERVICE CONTACT]]; privacy complaints may also be raised with the National Privacy Commission. These contacts must be completed before publication.
